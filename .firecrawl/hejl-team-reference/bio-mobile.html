Jakub Hejl

Chairman & Director
LinkedIn Website

Jakub Hejl is the Chairman and Director of the Hejl Foundation, which oversees his personal philanthropy. In this role, he shapes and approves foundation strategies, advocates for the foundation’s issues, and sets the organization’s overall direction. He works with grantees and partners to further the foundation’s goal of improving equity in the United States and around the world.

Raised in Florida, Mr. Hejl is a business entrepreneur who founded Westside Capital Group, a real estate investment firm based in Miami, FL. With a wealth of experience in real estate transactions, Mr. Hejl has successfully navigated transactions exceeding $4 billion in total value across various types in both the United States and Europe.

Mr. Hejl graduated with honors from the University of Miami School of Business, earning a business degree in Finance and Real Estate with a concentration in Economics. He is also an alumnus of the Hyperion Council, a charitable initiative led by the Dean of the School of Business at the University of Miami, dedicated to supporting underprivileged communities in the US and abroad. Additionally, he is a member of the University of Miami President’s Council and the Wolfsonian Advisory Board. Apart from his professional pursuits, Mr. Hejl has a keen interest in sports and politics.

Mr. Hejl was born in Prague, Czech Republic, but grew up in Florida. He currently resides in Miami, FL with his wife and three children.
