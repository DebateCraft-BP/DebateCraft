02

Programme objectives

Learning outcomes schools can assess.

Instruction moves from argument structure to competition performance. Coaches use practice speeches, round records, and formative feedback to identify the next skill to teach.

Foundations

Construct and explain a case

Students learn motion analysis, claims, reasons, evidence, impacts, signposting, and clear delivery.

  • Baseline speaking and argument assessment
  • Case construction and evidence evaluation
  • Short speeches with formative feedback
  • Reflection after each exercise

Competition

Apply skills in rounds

Students use rebuttal, points of information, weighing, clash, and role allocation under time limits.

  • Practice rounds and ballot review
  • WSDC or BP role preparation
  • Selection and squad development
  • Competition-cycle review

04

Delivery and assessment

Clear records for students and staff.

Live online sessions follow the school timetable and competition calendar. Coaches report attendance, participation, skill checkpoints, practice-round notes, and next targets.

Baseline assessment

Coaches record experience, speaking, argument structure, and format familiarity before instruction begins.

Differentiation

Exercises and feedback reflect student age, experience, language background, and squad role.

Formative feedback

Students receive specific feedback after speeches and practice rounds, linked to the next exercise.

Review and handover

Staff receive a concise progress record and curriculum notes for future cohorts.

05

Training evidence

Instruction, practice, review.

Students learn a concept, apply it in a timed exercise or practice round, then revise from feedback.

School programme enquiries

Discuss cohort, timetable, format, and competition goals.
