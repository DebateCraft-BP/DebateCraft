For schools considering a programme

Two practical starting points.

Schools can begin with an existing junior team or develop a new programme. In both cases, coaching follows the academic calendar and leaves a clear record for staff and future cohorts.

Mandate A

Train the junior team

Build confident first- and second-year debaters before poor habits harden.

  • Weekly squad training
  • Motion preparation and speaking roles
  • Feedback after every practice round
  • Competition-specific intensives

Launch a school club with curriculum, coach support, and a path into competition.

04

Operating model

Built for careful, long-term school work.

DebateCraft combines experienced competitors, small-group teaching, and reusable curriculum. The online model keeps delivery consistent across time zones and school terms.

School-shaped

Training follows term dates, competition calendars, student level, and staff capacity.

Junior-first

We build bench strength early instead of concentrating resources only on senior stars.

Transparent

Schools see scope, learning goals, delivery model, and coach responsibilities before launch.

Transferable

Students gain research, speaking, teamwork, and reasoning habits useful beyond debate.
